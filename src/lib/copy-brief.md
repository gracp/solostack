## 8. FAQ

**Section header:**
> Straight answers.

**Q1: "I already spend 20 minutes a day on social. Won't this just add another tool?"**
A: SoloStack replaces your current workflow, not adds to it. If you're spending 20 minutes a day posting without a strategy, you're working hard and getting inconsistent results. Our members report cutting their social media time to under 20 minutes a week — because the content is already made and queued before they open the app.

**Q2: "Why is this $29/month when Buffer is free?"**
A: Buffer is a calendar. SoloStack is a social media manager. You can't hire a human social media manager for $29/month. What you can do is use a tool that builds your strategy, writes your posts, adapts them for every platform, and schedules them — so you're not staring at a blank compose box every Tuesday morning. The comparison isn't the cost. The comparison is the time and output.

**Q3: "I don't want to hand my brand voice over to AI."**
A: We get it — and it's the right instinct. SoloStack learns from your input. You set your tone, goals, and topics. The AI generates drafts, not final orders. You review and approve every post before it goes live. Think of it as a very fast junior copywriter, not an autopilot. Your voice stays yours.

**Q4: "What happens to my data? I don't want my content训练的 into your AI."**
A: Your content is yours. We don't use it to train our models. Your posts, analytics, and brand inputs are used solely to run your account. We're building a business, not an AI training dataset. Full privacy terms will be published at launch — we're happy to share them early if you ask.

**Q5: "How long before I see results?"**
A: Most members post consistently for the first time within 48 hours of signing up. For results — more followers, engagement, leads — algos and audiences vary. What we can guarantee: you'll be posting consistently for the first time in your business. That alone puts you ahead of 80% of solopreneurs who start and stop social media every month.

**Q6: "Can I cancel anytime?"**
A: Monthly plans cancel anytime from your account settings — no emails, no calls, no guilt. Yearly plans have a 30-day refund window. We don't bury the cancel button. If SoloStack isn't working for you, we'd rather you leave on good terms than resent us.

## Copy Notes for Designers

- **Tone:** Direct, confident, specific. Not startup-cute. Not corporate-stiff. Think peer-to-peer advice from someone who built it.
- **Favicon:** Stack icon in indigo
- **CTA buttons:** Solid indigo (#4F46E5 or similar), white text, subtle shadow. Rounded corners (4px radius). No gradients.
- **Section spacing:** Generous. Let the white space breathe.
- **Typography:** Clean grotesque (Inter, DM Sans, or similar). Large, bold headlines. 16px minimum body text. No serif anywhere.
- **Color usage:** White background, charcoal (#1A1A1A or #111) for body text, indigo (#4F46E5) for accents and CTAs only. No secondary colors.
- **Icons:** Lucide or Phosphor, single weight, minimal. No emoji.
- **Social proof bar:** Horizontal pill/badge style. Indigo text on very light indigo background (#EEF2FF).
- **Pricing cards:** White cards, clean borders. Pro tier gets indigo top border or subtle background tint to signal "recommended."
- **Testimonial cards:** White card, quote in body text weight, name + role in smaller muted text, result metric in indigo bold.
- **FAQ:** Accordion style. Bold question, readable answer. No decorative icons needed.
- **No photography on MVP.** Illustrations or solid color blocks only if needed for visual pacing.
